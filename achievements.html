Achievements & Awards

Recognizing a lifetime of remarkable scientific contributions.

A Legacy of Excellence

Marie Curie's discoveries changed the history of science. Her work earned international recognition and opened new opportunities for women in scientific research.

1903

Nobel Prize in Physics

Marie Curie shared the Nobel Prize in Physics with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel for their research on radiation phenomena.

1911

Nobel Prize in Chemistry

She received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for her discovery of radium and polonium, and her investigation of radium and its compounds.

1906

First Woman Professor

She became the first woman to hold a professorship at the University of Paris after Pierre Curie's death.

1921

Recognition in the United States

During her visit to the United States, she received a gram of radium donated to support her scientific research.

1922

International Scientific Work

She became a member of the International Commission for Intellectual Cooperation, contributing to international scientific collaboration.

Historic Achievement

Two Nobel Prizes

She was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and remains the only person to receive Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields.
