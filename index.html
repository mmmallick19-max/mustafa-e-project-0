A Woman Who Changed Science

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist who became one of the most influential scientists in history.

She conducted groundbreaking research on radioactivity and, together with Pierre Curie, discovered two chemical elements: polonium and radium.

Her dedication, intelligence and determination opened new doors for women in scientific research and inspired generations of scientists.

A Legacy Beyond Discovery

Marie Curie's discoveries transformed scientific understanding of radioactive materials and opened new paths in physics, chemistry and medicine.

During World War I, she also helped develop mobile X-ray units to assist doctors in examining wounded soldiers.
