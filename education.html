Education & Learning

The academic journey behind a scientific legend.

Her Educational Journey

Marie Curie's education was shaped by determination, hard work, and a passion for knowledge. Despite the barriers faced by women in higher education, she continued pursuing science.

1867–1891

Early Education in Poland

Marie studied in Warsaw and developed a strong foundation in mathematics, physics, and other subjects. She also attended the underground Flying University, which offered educational opportunities to Polish students.

1891

University of Paris

Marie moved to Paris and enrolled at the Sorbonne, where she studied physics and mathematics.

1893

Physics Degree

She completed her degree in physics, graduating at the top of her class.

1894

Mathematics Degree

She earned a second degree in mathematics while continuing her scientific studies.

1903

Doctoral Research

Her doctoral research focused on radioactive substances and contributed to the development of modern nuclear science.
