Biography

The extraordinary life of a woman who changed science forever.

Who Was Marie Curie?

Marie Curie was a pioneering physicist and chemist whose research transformed our understanding of radioactivity. She became one of the most influential scientists in history.

She was born Maria Skłodowska on November 7, 1867, in Warsaw, Poland. At that time, Poland was under Russian rule. From an early age, she showed a remarkable interest in learning and science.

In 1891, she moved to Paris to study at the University of Paris, also known as the Sorbonne. There she studied physics and mathematics and later met Pierre Curie, who became her research partner and husband.

Marie Curie dedicated her life to scientific research. Her work led to the discovery of polonium and radium and helped establish the science of radioactivity.

Personal Details

Born: November 7, 1867

Birthplace: Warsaw, Poland

Nationality: Polish and French

Profession: Physicist and Chemist
